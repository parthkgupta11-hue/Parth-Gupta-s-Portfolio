Robotics · VR · Software

R1 VR Teleoperation

A Meta Quest 1 headset that drives the Unitree R1 humanoid's arms, head and walking, tested in Isaac Sim.

  • Built a WebXR page for the Quest 1 that streams head and hand tracking to a PC, which turns it into arm, waist and head joint commands for the R1 using inverse kinematics
  • Trained a walking policy in Isaac Lab with reinforcement learning so the R1 steps when the operator steps and stays balanced while the arms move
  • Added safety checks, including an arm self-collision guard, a hold when hand tracking drops and an e-stop
  • Ran the full system with the headset in Isaac Sim without falls; testing on the real robot is still to come

Electronics · PCB design

RC Firetruck

A remote controlled firetruck on a custom KiCad board, driven by an ESP32 and a PS5 controller.

  • Created a custom CAD model of a firetruck and 3D printed it to serve as the chassis
  • Developed a PCB that handles signal connections and power distribution to a variety of components:
    • Motor drivers (TB6612FNG, ULN2003A)
    • DC motors
    • Stepper motor
    • LEDs
    • Passive buzzer
    • ESP32 microcontroller
    • Voltage converters (5 V and 3.3 V)
  • Used capacitors, inductors and diodes to smooth the power supply across the board and reduce the risk of damaging components
  • Hand soldered everything on the board, including the 0402 SMD components
  • Connected a PS5 controller to the ESP32 over Bluetooth and wrote the code in Arduino IDE, then flashed it to the microcontroller

Robotics · 3D printing

Robotic Arm

A 3D printed arm with servo joints and a gripper, modeled in OnShape and powered by a custom KiCad board that is still in design.

  • Recently started project
  • Modeled the entire arm in OnShape, using mates and motor imports to check that it moves properly
  • Removed material from non-stressed and under-stressed parts to reduce the torque the motors need
  • Assembled the arm after 3D printing, using printed axles for movement and fasteners to lock the motors in place
  • Uses one NEMA stepper motor, four large MG995 servos and one MG90 servo
  • Created the schematic for a power distribution PCB that supplies the motors and the ESP32 microcontroller
  • Plans to manufacture and assemble the PCB, then add ROS 2 and other libraries to expand what the arm can do
